# shuttle-replay

```{image} assets/header.svg
:alt: shuttle-replay
:width: 100%
```

**Turn a local badminton video, your detections and your review into a
side-by-side court replay. Offline.**

`shuttle-replay` calibrates a camera from court points you reviewed, fits
gravity + quadratic-drag flights to the 2D shuttle detections you supply, and
renders an MP4 with the source video beside a top-view court.

It is a research and review tool, not a line-calling system, shot classifier,
shuttle detector or measured 3D speed gun:

- **You supply the detections.** There is no bundled shuttle or player detector
  and no model weights are downloaded.
- **You supply the review.** Contacts, landing regions and calibration points
  are reviewed by a person; nothing is found or repaired automatically.
- **3D is conditional, not measured.** Every 3D position and speed is a physics
  model output that depends on your calibration and annotations. There is no
  learned depth, and low reprojection error is not proof of 3D accuracy.
